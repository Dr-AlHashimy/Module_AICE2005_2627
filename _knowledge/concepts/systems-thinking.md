# Systems Thinking

## Definition

Systems thinking is a theoretical and analytical lens used to understand the behaviour of complex systems. It originates from systems theory (e.g. von Bertalanffy) and emphasises how parts of a system interact through feedback loops, interdependencies, and emergent behaviours, rather than analysing each part in isolation. 

Key characteristics:

- Focus on the whole system, not just its parts
- Identifies feedback loops, delays, and non-linear cause-effect relationships
- Helps in understanding why systems behave as they do over time
- Often used for diagnosis, modelling, and scenario planning

Systems thinking underpins systemic design (systems thinking applied to design practice) and is applied specifically to AI systems in "Systems Thinking for AI Engineers".
