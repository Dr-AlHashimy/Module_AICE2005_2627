# Monolithic Design

## Definition

Monolithic design approaches attempt to optimise all design variables simultaneously in a single, tightly integrated process. This has the advantage of potentially achieving a globally optimal solution, since no information is lost to decomposition, but it suffers from scalability issues and coordination challenges as system complexity grows — every variable must be considered together, which becomes intractable for large multi-component systems. It is one pole of the central design choice systematic design must resolve, contrasted with distributed design.
